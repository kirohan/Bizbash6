CASE FILE / ABOUT

What is BizBash?

BizBash 6.0 is the signature fresher event of KUET Career Club (KCC), created to introduce first-year students to case solving and problem analysis. Since 2019, it has provided a platform for freshers to think critically, solve real-world problems, showcase their potential, build confidence, and sharpen problem-solving skills from the very start of campus life.

EVIDENCE / RULES

Registration Guidelines

  • Each team must consist of three (3) members.
  • Members may be from the same or different KUET departments.
  • All team members must be from Batch 2K25.
  • A participant cannot be a member of more than one team.
  • Open only to full-time undergraduate students currently enrolled at KUET.
  • Registration is conducted online.
  • Non-refundable registration fee: BDT 550 per team.
  • Registration deadline: September 15, 2026 at 11:59 PM.
  • The team leader receives a confirmation email after successful registration and payment.
  • Rule violations may result in penalty or immediate disqualification.

OPERATION / ROUNDS

Crack the Case

Two stages. One mission: make your strategy impossible to ignore.

ROUND 01

Case Solution

Submit by 21 September 2026 · 11:59 PM

  • The team leader receives the case and submission link via email.
  • Submit the presentation in both PPTX and PDF formats.
  • Maximum 15 slides; team name must appear on slide one.
  • Cover problem identification, solution, vision, target audience, market analysis, competitors, action plan, timeline, revenue model, financial analysis, and contingency plan.
FINAL ROUND

Live Pitch

2 October 2026 · KUET, Khulna

  • Physical final round at Khulna University of Engineering & Technology.
  • Report one hour before the program begins for screening.
  • A new case is provided after announcement of the 1st Round results.
  • Submit slides in PPTX and PDF format; no slide limit.
  • 7 minutes to pitch + 3 minutes for Q&A.
